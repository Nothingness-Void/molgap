# Retrieved Platform Records

Retained platform outputs, manifests, acceptance records, costs, and provenance.

Keep compact canonical receipts, manifests, acceptance, costs and provenance in
Git. Large downloaded payloads are operation inputs with separately verified
durable custody; a historical reference does not require every duplicate local
copy to remain resident. Retire redundant payloads after checking active users,
hashes, retrievability and local availability claims. Preserve sole evidence.
The [checkout and artifact lifetime](../../BRANCHES.md#local-checkout-and-artifact-lifetime)
rule owns cleanup; archive merges alone do not preserve ignored files.

- Platform boundaries and durability rules: [platforms README](../README.md).
- Provider indexes: [Kaggle](kaggle/README.md), [IMS](ims/README.md), and [SCNet](scnet/README.md).
