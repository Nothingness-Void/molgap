## Decision and causal limits

- Retain the diagnostic evidence and close this bounded run.
- Do not claim gate failure, source collapse, padding defect or disconnected
  aggregation from these observations. The prior portability uncertainty and
  consideration gate remain unchanged.
- Do not launch source dropout merely because the paper supplied a mechanism.
  The assay failed to establish the proposed cohort-collapse rationale; source
  regularization can remain a separately motivated research hypothesis, not a
  validated repair. No training/scale/extra-seed successor was released.
- A future more discriminating question would measure functional reliance at
  predeclared late checkpoints or test structurally meaningful relation encoding
  under a separately frozen contract. Checkpoint snapshots and reused512-row
  panels cannot isolate training-time overfitting or explain the full100K→500K
  attenuation. Neither conditional recommendation grants compute authority.
