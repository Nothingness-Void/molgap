## Primary methods/code rechecked

### TGT: keep distinct operations distinct

[Method3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.04538v2#S3.SS1) describes scalar-gated
triplet aggregation and heavier query-dependent triplet attention. Its dropout
operates on interaction weights; our closed whole-molecule branch dropout was
not that operation. Geometry prediction/pretraining also contributed to the
published system. We neither import those stages nor treat our direct-Gap
addon as its full reproduction. Weight-level dropout remains technically
untested here, but one paper difference alone is insufficient to reopen a
regularization campaign after three unfavorable aggregate follow-ups.

### OpenFold triangle multiplication: a real implementation difference

The [author implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aqlaboratory/openfold/main/openfold/model/triangular_multiplicative_update.py),
`TriangleMultiplicativeUpdate.forward`, separately gates input projections,
contracts them, applies output LayerNorm, projects and uses a target-pair output
gate. Our closed product retained the aggregate's scalar routing and did not
copy that whole operation. Its failure therefore does not falsify every
normalized triangular update. This is a code-level contrast, **not** evidence
that missing normalization caused our error: finite active returns and their
logged RMS do not establish explosion. Protein results do not predict PCQM
gain. A normalized-product rescue stays lower priority until a bounded
diagnostic supports its particular causal deficiency; no copied Evoformer,
protein data, pretrained weights or geometry is proposed.

### DGT: persistent bond tokens, but not a qualified reproduction

The [official DGT layer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zhangsy-ryan/DGT/main/graphgps/layer/dgt_layer.py)
updates separate atom `batch.x` and bond `batch.e` streams with residual
attention/FFN paths. The [author repository](https://github.com/zhangsy-ryan/DGT)
also advertises graph topology/structural and optional3D inputs. This supports
considering a distinct bond state, not equating a transient node-directed MLP
with a complete dual-graph model. Publisher methods/PDF and accepted-version
retrieval were not reliably available in this review; full ablation/config
audit remains incomplete. No published accuracy/cost is transferred to our
proposal, and no geometry, graph-tool stack or full bond attention is imported.

### GRIT: an independent family, not an RRWP plugin

[Paper3.1–3.3](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/ma23c/ma23c.pdf) and the
[official layer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LiamMa/GRIT/main/grit/layer/grit_layer.py)
combine learned RRWP, nonlinear pair-conditioned attention/pair updates,
relation-aware values, degree scalers and normalization choices. A failed
RRWP adapter is not a complete GRIT trial. Conversely, copying only the
attention name does not reproduce its mechanisms. A faithful compact variant
needs accepted RRWP representation, correct real-bond degrees, normalization/
EMA-buffer semantics and measured FP32BS128 cost. That integration/contract
work remains larger than the proposed carry test, so GRIT stays a separate
family reserve rather than an immediate training release.

## Ranked work, not a new module catalogue

1. **Qualification prerequisite:** make the existing full50K audit actually
   support exact frozen variants/reference, label-after-prediction persistence,
   reproduction, chunks, roles/costs and separate NO_TRAIN closure. This enables
   meaningful future portability checks; it does not generate an artificial
   transfer win or require retraining closed100K experiments.
2. **First model hypothesis:** retain the strong local+aggregate reference and
   add only per-directed-bond depth carry to its existing64-channel message.
   No hard mask, new attention, extra width or readout change. The proposed
   full-model parameter count is an algebraic5,880,972, not a measured inventory.
   Disabled-carry encoder equivalence, all11gradient paths, exact parent
   initialization and runtime/source/RML qualification must precede compute.
3. **Independent family reserve:** compact complete GRIT, after input/recipe
   compatibility and budget qualification. Do not use a weak or unmatched
   comparator to manufacture a win.
4. **Lower-priority mechanism reserve:** contraction normalization/output
   gating, only after evidence supports that specific problem. Do not relabel
   the failed raw-product run as a positive normalized-product result.

The carry primitive passed synthetic identity, parameter/RNG inventory,
autograd, bounded coefficients, permutation, reset, shape/dtype and empty-edge
checks. Its design intentionally has no permanently disconnected first-layer
coefficient and stores no previous molecule state in the module. These are
unit properties, not encoder preflight, a learning result or Replay readiness.
