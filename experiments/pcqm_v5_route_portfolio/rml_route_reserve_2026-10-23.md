# RML evidence analysis and literature reserve — 2026-10-23

## Scope and decision

This was a local evidence review and primary-source literature search, not an
experiment release. It analyzed the actual working-tree RML source records,
reconstructed admitted curve comparisons in memory, and read the later Modern2D
decisions separately. It did not train/infer a model, load graph datasets, query
remote jobs, rebuild derived RML, modify historical conclusions, or implement a
candidate. The fixed PCQM assets and protected-role boundaries were unchanged.

**The evidence supports a narrower research order, not another broad addon sweep.**
Real-bond GPTrans communication has the strongest retained 500K evidence. The
missing long-budget removal control is more informative than another parameter
change. For new architectures, lightweight topology encoding in ESA is a
reasonable conditional reserve; semantic auxiliary supervision is a weaker,
objective-specific reserve. Neither is an established PCQM improvement or an
authorized GPU candidate. Content-conditioned PPGT and count-conditioned ESA
already belong to a separately released screen and must not be proposed again.

### Interpretation discipline

Only admitted identical worlds are paired, at identical optimizer steps and
sample presentations. Positive gain below means reference minus candidate MAE.
Curve-selected minima are producer reductions; independently recomputed saved
prediction endpoints can differ in the last decimal places. Online train,
live-dev and EMA-dev are different measurement modes. Row bootstrap does not
measure seed variation. Neither a different role nor a shortened cosine horizon
can be silently treated as the same comparison. No historical early-stop/STQS
rule was used to veto scale transfer.

### 2.2 Parent-dependent utility explains apparently contradictory modules

On K1, source-built40-point comparisons include atom-pair local -0.002464eV,
bond-type local -0.001580, sparse persistent triplet -0.001119 and motif hierarchy
-0.000342. RRWP PairToken (+0.002449), one-shot triplet PairToken (+0.002607),
functional-group token (+0.001542) and RWSE refresh (+0.001007) were directional
but did not establish the required portable material gain. The independent
MetaGIN adaptation lost0.022814eV despite improved training fit.

K1 already carries real-bond memory and topology-related structure. GPTrans's
local/triplet success is not evidence that K1 needs another copy of that
information. Conversely, K1's unsuccessful RWSE *refresh* does not falsify
adding initially absent position information to ESA. **The incremental missing
computation relative to the parent is the experimental unit, not a module name.**
Redundancy is a plausible explanation, not an identified causal mechanism.

### 2.3 Scale failure has at least two observed components

The [frozen PairToken diagnostic](../pcqm_k1_cross_scale_frozen/decision.md)
reproduced the original100K predictions, then observed the same frozen pair's
gain fall from0.003044 to0.000416eV on later internal molecules, before any
500K retraining. The subsequently500K-trained matched pair reversed to
-0.000699eV on those rows. Simple atom-count/conjugation marginal shifts did
not explain most attenuation. Training exposure alone therefore cannot explain
the entire loss. Selection optimism, joint cohort changes and training-contract
effects remain unresolved; descriptive70/30 decompositions are not causal shares.

The older [PairToken terminal decision](../pcqm_k1_pair_token_500k/results/decision_122312462.md)
used a different retained K1 scalar and recorded a +0.000556eV point gain, below
its0.003 gate, with strict comparison pending. Do not overwrite that historical
NEGATIVE_UNDER_CONTRACT label or confuse it with “every molecule got worse.”
Do not silently substitute its scalar reference for the later aligned comparison.

Triplet aggregate similarly retained only0.001767eV on its later10K frozen
panel, versus0.003050 originally; its row interval crossed zero. The
[source assay](../pcqm_gptrans_source_dependence/decision_terminal.md) did not
find a broad source/gate collapse. The
[portability decision](../pcqm_gptrans_triplet_portability/decision_terminal.md)
does not qualify another500K training run. None of this establishes a universal
rule that100K MAE can automatically veto500K.

### 2.4 Pretraining failed specific allocation/target tests, not all SSL

The [same-database source-task study](../pcqm_gap_architecture/results/pretraining_dual_account_seed42/decision.md)
found an apparent40-epoch benefit disappear against longer scratch training;
nominally identical scratch40 runs differed by about0.002692eV. Aggregate
composition/fragment source tasks did not establish accepted transfer. Geometry
histogram denoising was negative and is not reopened under the present boundary.

The [local-hierarchy20+20 study](../pcqm_gap_architecture/results/local_hierarchy_pretraining_seed42/decision.md)
accelerated early Gap fitting but ended0.000235eV worse than scratch40. Half its
encoder passes lacked Gap supervision. A learnable auxiliary loss is not proof
of downstream value. That historical BS48 contract is also not a V5 BS128
reference. K1 corruption-only lost0.000352eV; corruption+atom reconstruction
gained0.002860 but stayed below gate. These results favor precise supervision
and equal-total-cost controls, not repeating a generic masking/pretraining grid.

### 2.5 Complementarity exists; learned routing is not established

The [saved-prediction combination study](../pcqm_specialist_combination_transfer/decision.md)
retained a fixed equal K1/PairToken average with0.002538eV exploratory500K gain
over its stronger single model. Learned global/bin weights added no value.
Both roles were reused; no independent confirmation or measured two-encoder
deployment cost exists. This supports retaining a fixed-blend hypothesis, not a
label-oracle router, a new specialist, or self-distillation under a teacher ban.

## 3. Modern2D changes the research priorities, but not the comparison world

### PPGT: readout sensitivity and execution were real; final quality is unresolved

The [four-arm partial analysis](../modern2d_configured_100k/research_arms/ppgt1/results/interpretation.md)
matched all arms at pass10/7,810 updates, rather than unequal paused cursors:

|---|---:|---:|
| Sum | 0.2779392600 | 0.2852467299 |
| Mean | 0.2404640019 | 0.2334533930 |

Mean improved both observed train and dev behavior without reducing parameter
count. Increasing LayerScale hurt sum but helped mean: it is not a universally
beneficial residual fix. The prefix cannot distinguish convergence acceleration
from a better terminal solution. All arms stopped for cost, not OOM; the retained
four-update capture contained130,250 CUDA kernel launches. Parameter count alone
does not predict cost or usable optimization exposure.

The later [prepared pair](../modern2d_configured_100k/prepared_pair/results/decision.md)
completed20 passes and independently accepted PPGT mean at0.2004566171eV.
However, prepared PPGT's early trajectory differed from the older execution
history by up to about0.02837eV despite passing its tolerance-based native gate.
Do not splice that20-pass endpoint onto the old10-pass curves to infer a causal
pooling/LayerScale trajectory. A new intervention must bind its actual backend.

### ESA: LR mattered much more than the residual replacement

The [completed ESA factorial](../modern2d_configured_100k/research_arms/esa2/results/interpretation.md)
found identity-residual gains of+0.000989eV at LR3e-4 and-0.000807eV at1e-4;
row intervals did not establish a consistent effect. Changing LR3e-4→1e-4
worsened selected errors by0.01560–0.01740eV under the same20-pass horizon.
This makes another unconditional LN/identity swap a weak next question. It does
not prove that ever-higher LR, longer schedules or larger models would help.

Code inspection found native ESA tokens built from endpoint categorical atom
features, categorical bonds and isolate-token type; attention has incidence M
and all-real-token S masks. It found **no explicit RWSE/RRWP channel** in those
inputs. This is a concrete representation difference to investigate, not proof
that ESA has no topology: its M masks already encode shared endpoints.
Owners: [features](../../src/molgap/esa_features.py),
[native model](../../src/molgap/esa_native.py).

## 5. Ranked route reserve and the smallest informative contrasts

Grades refer to evidence, not predicted probability of success:
**A** = accepted local matched positive within its stated scope;
**B** = relevant controlled literature plus a concrete missing local mechanism;
**C** = indirect-task/mechanistic evidence or unresolved reproducibility.
C/C+ items are watchlist questions, **not eligible additions to a submit-ready
experimental matrix**. Their missing evidence must be supplied first. B supports
a falsifiable reserve, not a probability-of-success claim. No row grants
implementation, new role access or compute.

### R2: endpoint RWSE design and why it is different

Proposed adaptation, not a paper reproduction: compute node return probabilities
for a fixed short walk range from the accepted adjacency, and project endpoint
summaries into ESA's existing token width. Preserve directed endpoint ordering;
atom-only tokens need an explicit missing-endpoint convention. Do not zero out
existing chemical tokens, remove global keys or alter M/S/PMA. Begin with a
function-preserving zero return, but verify later gradient connectivity rather
than treating initialization equality as usefulness.

Keep LR3e-4, precision, seed42, BS128, backend and schedule identical to the
qualified reference. Derive only from accepted topology; any feature sidecar
needs explicit identity/CPU acceptance and separate authority, not a graph-cache
rebuild. A future positive needs a material paired endpoint and late-curve benefit,
not just larger PE RMS or a favorable early checkpoint. Persistent cost increase
must be reported. Same-family baseline/feature and size-stratified saved-output
contrasts are missing; cross-family scores cannot supply them.

### R3: supervise a useful relation without spending away Gap exposure

The proposed distinction is clean Gap prediction **plus** a small local
motif/correspondence objective, not corrupting the primary Gap input or spending
half the budget before Gap training. Targets must be deterministic from allowed
training topology, with vocabulary/statistics fitted on training members only.
No external teacher, privileged geometry or unseen auxiliary labels are assumed.

Paper motif labels and local K1 group-token failures concern different interventions:
auxiliary loss versus an extra forward pathway. That difference justifies keeping
the question, not calling it likely positive. Match total measured cost as well
as supervised updates; if both cannot be matched, declare which resource is held
constant and retain the other as a measured confound. Reject a claimed semantic
gain if shuffled assignments achieve the same effect. The already paused
GPTrans correspondence implementation is the first evidence to inspect, not an
excuse for another overlapping candidate.

### R4: only after cardinality attribution

PPGT already consumes log1p(N) and inverse-order pair features; another graph-size
MLP is not automatically new information there. ESA's count-conditioned readout
is already under test. Only if count effects fail to explain a retained residual
should a tiny composition/ring/conjugation branch be considered. Its descriptor
definition, train-only scaling and incremental information beyond the base model
must be established first. Earlier weak residual correlations, failed routing
and aggregate-source pretraining prevent promoting fingerprints by popularity.

## 7. Do not reopen these routes without genuinely new evidence

- Generic width/depth expansion, coefficient grids, unconditional normalization
  swaps, repeated slot/query/global gates, and path+G1 additive assumptions.
- Raw triplet products, hard bond-only support, whole-branch drop, local caps or
  K1 local-capacity coloring as disguised new candidates.
- Motif/line-graph/RWSE labels without identifying what is absent from the parent.
- Generic atom reconstruction, graph histograms or external checkpoint pretraining
  without an equal-total-resource, target-relevant counterfactual.
- Molecular routing selected by label oracle or target-ranked “hard molecules.”
- Treating engineering speedups,32-step workflow trials, missing canonical RML
  finalization, or STOP_FOR_COST prefixes as model-quality outcomes.

OGB's public PCQM rules and this project's stricter pure2D/teacher boundary are
not the same thing. [OGB documentation](https://ogb.stanford.edu/docs/lsc/pcqm4mv2/)
permits training geometries for2D inference; the present project roadmap does
not thereby authorize them. The reserve keeps the existing database and accepted
memberships. It does not assert a2D irreducible-error floor from unmeasured
label/conformer ambiguity.
