The reference capture included138,576 cudaLaunchKernel calls,80,940 fill calls,

and constructs native_tokens independently for each graph before padding.
Per-graph categorical one-hot, isolated-atom nonzero/indexing and repeated

does not separately prove the cost of each source function. A token-preparation
or graph-view optimization must preserve directed bonds, isolated atoms,
padding/masks and exact state/update/resume semantics before claiming speedup.

The observed pass time makes another unchanged60-pass attempt infeasible
inside the same8-hour allocation. This is a budget-feasibility warning, not a
forecast of final MAE. It argues against another unchanged long retry before
execution attribution and equivalence qualification.

## Decision

The slot was closed without continuation, extra seed,500K or successor.
Weight decay alone was not demonstrated to rescue ESA within the allowed
budget. Its small late partial advantage was retained as a hypothesis, not
promoted. The next useful decision depended on the separate LR attribution
and on addressing measured forward/tokenization overhead, not on spending
another unchanged training allocation. The LR notebook remained under its
own Luna binding and authority.
