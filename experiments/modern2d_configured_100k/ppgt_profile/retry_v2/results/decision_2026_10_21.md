for this batch. Neither the capture nor worker elapsed time is steady-state

## Decision

The useful hypothesis was batch-vectorized execution of the same graphwise
mathematics, with outputs, gradients, optimizer update and resume behavior
checked before any performance claim. Disabling positional features, changing
precision, widening the model or launching500K was not supported by this profile.
Any such follow-up required a separately frozen bounded authority. This
diagnostic released no automatic successor. Its terminal RML classification was
`NO_TRAIN/context_only`, with full billing explicitly missing rather than zero.
The two separately authorized ESA attribution notebooks were unaffected.
