# Stage-2 frozen-checkpoint causal audit

## Question

Which of K1's layer-3, layer-6, or layer-9 global exchanges causes the paired
deficit on the topology-extreme molecular strata identified by Stage 1?

## Frozen intervention

- Load the accepted K1-v4 seed-42 checkpoint without changing any weight.
- Reproduce its frozen 50K development payload on Kunshan before accepting any
  counterfactual.
- Evaluate the unchanged checkpoint with layer 3, 6, or 9 suppressed one at a
  time and with all three global updates suppressed. Local EdgeState operations,
  pooling, head, data order, FP32 precision, and physical inference batch 128
  remain unchanged.
- Measure assignment entropy/effective atom count, maximum assignment mass,
  update-to-hidden RMS, and node-dispersion change at every exchange.
- Report paired absolute-error changes for the Stage-1 topology-extreme strata
  and their middle control.

This is model inference on the reused official-train-derived development role.
It performs no training and reads no official validation or test role.

## Three-round authority

1. This frozen layer-ablation audit is round 1.
2. Round 2 may vary only the strength/direction of the single exchange layer
   implicated by round 1, using the same checkpoint and no training.
3. Round 3 may train at most one minimal nested repair derived directly from
   rounds 1 and 2 under the unchanged PCQM-100K v4 contract.

If a round does not identify a coherent bottleneck, the remaining rounds are
not spent blindly. A trained round-3 candidate still needs at least `0.003 eV`
gain over frozen K1-v4; this authorization does not release seeds 43/44,
shadow, scale-up, official validation, test-dev, or full training.
