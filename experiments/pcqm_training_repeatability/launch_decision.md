# Training Repeatability Launch Decision

The same-allocation, same-seed Kaggle T4x2 replay is authorized as a bounded
measurement of technical training drift. It does not compare architectures,
read sealed roles, or authorize promotion.

The frozen submission identity is `kaggle_t4x2/kernel-metadata.json`; the exact
training and acceptance gates are in `protocol.md`. Terminal metrics and a
final decision must be added only after both replicas are retrieved and pass
`accept.py`.
