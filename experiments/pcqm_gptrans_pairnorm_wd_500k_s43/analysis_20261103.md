# Follow-up: why stronger WD did not improve the500K endpoint

Retained-artifact interpretation only. No training, checkpoint inference, new role
access or change to the frozen decisions. Companion: ../pcqm_gptrans_pairnorm_wd_500k_s44/.

## Observed trade-off

WD0.10 minus0.01 development EMA MAE at pass10 is+6.505/+6.618meV
for initializations43/44, and at pass20 is+7.158/+7.524meV. The deficit
starts early, so it cannot be explained solely by a late noisy endpoint.
Selected-to-final drift falls from0.437/0.468meV to0.172/0.202meV,
but selected MAE is worse by0.604/1.131meV. A smoother tail is not a better optimum.
Stronger WD selects pass53/52 versus43/37. Final normalized online TRAIN
objectives are about0.071 versus0.055; these are not fixed-weight EMA TRAIN MAEs.
The evidence supports slower fitting under stronger shrinkage within the fixed
60-pass contract, not a proof of an architectural ceiling or universal underfitting.

## Decay application and accumulated strength

src/molgap/family_training_kernel.py:64-72,94 constructs AdamW directly on
model.parameters(), with no separate zero-decay group. Biases and learned
normalization parameters therefore share the same declared decay.
ContractSchedule.before_pass fixes LR for each pass. From the retained60-pass
trace, sum_t(lr_t)=119.242368 across234360 updates. The decay-only product
prod_t(1-lr_t*WD) is0.303483458 at WD0.01,0.002574163 at0.05,
and0.000006624827 at0.10. These are mathematical multipliers with the gradient
contribution separated out, not measured parameter norms or fractions of learned
information retained. Parameters without gradients on a step need not be decayed.
Under the same pass-wise schedule and BS128,500K has3906updates/pass versus781
at100K: cumulative decay log-strength is about5x as large. This identifies a
plausible cross-scale recipe mismatch; it does not prove that WD caused all earlier
100K-to500K transfer failures, particularly in another model family.
No parameter-group ablation or norm/gradient history identifies which tensors
are responsible. Norm/bias-specific damage remains a hypothesis.

## Explanations constrained by retained predictions

The existing post-hoc optimal constant-shift diagnosis leaves the high-minus-low
deficit at0.644/1.131meV instead of0.604/1.131meV. A global offset does not
remove it. This is same-development-role fitted analysis, not a deployment correction.
High-WD row win fractions are49.06%/50.088%; the worse average is not evidence
that every molecule got worse. Both paired intervals are positive and both
initializations share the direction; two inits still do not quantify full seed variance.

Historical matched-initialization WD0.05 MAEs are101.0866/101.3195meV,
versus102.2890/101.7287 at0.01 and102.8933/102.8597 at0.10. The middle setting
is descriptively better, but it was trained in other sessions and this is not a
three-level same-job causal experiment. It does not establish a universal optimum.

## Decision implication

Do not replace the retained recipe with WD0.10 or infer that extra epochs will
recover a better endpoint. More exposure has not been tested here; missing weight
and gradient trajectories preclude a unique mechanism. The tested change has
already answered its bounded question and does not nominate another WD grid.
The operational lesson is to compare cumulative schedule/step/decay strength,
not only nominal epoch count and the WD coefficient, when considering scale transfer.
