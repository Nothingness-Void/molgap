source and input identities before acceptance. Family `inspect_output` and native
preflight validators checked retained contracts, repeat/resume/profile proofs,
complete exposure, selected/resume states and aligned finite development rows.

trained arms. Each pair closure took about 106 seconds. Thin local assembly
selected existing APIs and translated records; no new trainer, RML validator or
dual-arm framework was introduced. Saved-prediction analysis reused
`molgap.router.paired_bootstrap_mean`; it ran no model or checkpoint inference.

All four trained terminal records are VALID/COMPLETE: two CLOSED controls and
two NEGATIVE_UNDER_CONTRACT candidates. Four original infrastructure failures
were independently finalized NO_TRAIN with the existing finalizer. Their
original prospective records remain immutable. Whole-notebook bootstrap cost is
recorded once per failed notebook; a separate candidate cost is unknown rather
than invented or double counted.

Strict training replay remains excluded for all four trained records. The raw
canonical traces have no learning-rate or checkpoint-identity histories, and
strict V5 comparison qualification is absent. Mechanical acceptance and terminal
publication do not establish replay readiness. The unchanged global frozen check
is the derived-index gate; it does not prove portable historical artifact custody.
Inherited local-retention claims remain outside this retrieval scope.
