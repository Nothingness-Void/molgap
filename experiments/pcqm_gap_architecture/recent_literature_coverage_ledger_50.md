## Reading-depth definitions

- **Configuration:** method, results/ablations, and task-specific model or
  training settings were checked in the primary paper, supplement, or official
  configuration.
- **Mechanism:** method and results/ablations were checked, but the source did
  not expose a sufficiently complete task-specific recipe or the recipe is not
  comparable to direct PCQM Gap prediction.

The ledger contains exactly **195 audited sources**: 194 research papers (77
configuration-level reads and 117 mechanism/teacher-level reads) plus one
critical post-publication audit. A missing number is therefore a ledger error,
not an invitation to infer an unreviewed paper.

## Coverage conclusion

The additional papers do not overturn the experiment queue. They make its
reasoning sharper:

1. direct controlled PCQM evidence now makes global-attention frequency an open
   budget-allocation question: strong local encoders can outperform larger GPS
   variants, so a local-heavy sparse-global schedule precedes another full-GPS
   expansion;
2. equivariant high-order models repeatedly require substantially more compute,
   and their largest gains often occur on forces or large angularly diverse
   systems rather than small-molecule scalar properties;
3. non-covalent through-space contacts remain an untested information channel,
   but current support is confounded by simultaneous geometry and decomposition
   changes; they require a separate CPU edge-statistics gate;
4. channelized Cartesian moments offer a more credible bounded fallback than
   another explicit tuple hierarchy when computation is the limiting factor;
5. geometry denoising and transferable potentials are credible teacher or
   conformer-improvement routes, but must not be misreported as random-init
   architecture gains;
6. the active deterministic ring hierarchy remains the current experiment and
   must finish before either new mechanism is implemented.
7. hypergraph results require representation-matched audits: MHNN supplies a
   direct but nonstandard PCQM signal, while its post-publication critique
   prevents treating higher-order connections as a standalone causal gain;
8. TGF-M and EquiHGNN show that geometry-conditioned fields and conjugated
   hyperedges remain interesting, but neither authorizes a change to the
   current random-init, 2D, official-role contract.
9. GoMS reinforces the value of arrangement-aware substructure relationships,
   but its internally inconsistent PCQM tables are a concrete reminder that
   reported numbers require cross-table and task-role auditing before use.
10. SIMG provides the clearest recent example of a chemistry-specific latent
    representation: orbital and lone-pair relations are learned first and then
    consumed by a downstream graph model. It is promising for a teacher or
    auxiliary-label track, but cannot be counted as a graph-only architecture
    improvement without the extra NBO supervision.
11. GPS++ provides a matched PCQM ablation showing that edge-aware local
    message passing, outgoing/sender aggregation and global summaries matter
    independently; its 2D MPNN-only result is better than its 2D hybrid. This
    strengthens the local-heavy schedule without justifying a larger GPS copy.
12. Uni-Mol+ reports atom--pair communication and pair--pair triangular
    updates alongside supervised conformation refinement. It is evidence for
    bottom-level interaction, not evidence that a late SchNet prediction
    fusion should be reopened or that a random-init 2D transplant is proven.
13. GAPE reports topology-matched graph-alignment pretraining and a compact
    downstream Transformer, with GAPE+RWPE outperforming the compared 2D
    positional encodings on PCQM. Because the PE is pretrained and the test
    Transformer omits edge features, this is a separate structural-pretraining
    track rather than an architecture-screen result.
14. Molecular Graph Transformer and the graph-free Transformer study converge
    on an information-flow lesson despite different domains: local interactions
    should dominate early processing, while global attention is most useful as
    a later adaptive correction. Both also warn that global attention is the
    expensive part; this strengthens the sparse/late schedule hypothesis.
15. Set-enhanced readout remains a low-cost orthogonal candidate: replacing
    mean pooling with a permutation-invariant RepSet layer improved SR-GINE
    over GINE in the authors' quantum-property benchmarks with only a small
    measured runtime increase, but it has no direct PCQM evidence and must not
    replace the accepted atom/bond information path by assumption.
16. The 2026 attentive-message-passing preprint isolates a useful local rule:
    bidirectional edge-aware messages plus degree-robust scatter-max improve
    over simpler message passing, while extra attention heads do not. This is
    a mechanism reference, not direct PCQM evidence or a reason to add a full
    global attention stack.
17. 3DMSE's ablations show that distances, angles, dihedrals, equivariant
    filters and hierarchical pooling each matter on its small QM9/PubChem
    setting, but the descriptor order and molecule-size limit make it a 3D
    information-flow reference rather than a bounded PCQM candidate.
18. MotiL shows that motif-aware twin-graph alignment is most useful when
    paired with diffusion priming and pretraining; it does not establish that
    a randomly initialized motif module will improve PCQM Gap.
19. Multi-channel structural pretraining separates molecule, scaffold and
    context objectives, while also acknowledging 2D's conformation/chirality
    limit. It supports semantic channel separation as a later teacher idea,
    not another in-contract fusion branch.
20. The NeurIPS GNN-pretraining study is a necessary negative control: a
    pretraining gain is not credible without matched scratch, feature, split,
    frozen-probe, and fine-tuning controls.
21. ET-OREO makes force-centric geometry pretraining attractive as a teacher
    objective, but its force labels and external conformers cannot be silently
    translated into the ETKDG-only scalar Gap route.
22. JMP demonstrates disciplined named-task multi-domain pretraining and
    public artifact packaging, while CSI suggests that task alignment can beat
    raw scale. Neither establishes a PCQM Gap result or justifies importing an
    external corpus.
23. The only database-preserving hypothesis admitted for later protocol design
     is train-role-only alignment selection within PCQM4Mv2 or repaired-2M, with
     equal-budget random and full-pool controls and sealed official roles.
24. O_SMI-SSM-336M demonstrates a complete SMILES-foundation and deployment
    artifact surface, but its external 91M-PubChem corpus and 336M scale do not
    imply that a sequence foundation model is the right PCQM bottleneck.
25. HieGT's AGA/MGA ablation supports separating local motif processing from
    cross-motif communication, while its DFT/RDKit geometry means the portable
    question is only a bounded 2D motif-mask comparator.
26. MolCHG is the cleanest new same-database hypothesis: fragment, topology,
    scaffold, and atom--bond source tasks can be generated from the permitted
    train role without importing external rows, but the hypothesis still
    requires scratch, no-auxiliary, and frozen-probe controls.
27. BiScale-GTR makes the learned-fragment alternative concrete through
    Graph-BPE and masked fragment reconstruction, but tokenizer determinism,
    OOV recursion, and checkpoint provenance require a CPU acceptance gate
    before any GPU screen.
28. FragNet's four-level graph and masked-fragment attribution are useful for
    interpretability and connection diagnostics, while Uni-Mol/RDKit 3D
    pretraining keeps its reported benchmark outside the current contract.
29. MORE is the strongest control-design reference in this batch because it
    separates linear probing, fine-tuning, pretext ablations, and scale; its
    ZINC/MMFF source roles must be replaced rather than imported.
30. FragmentNet shows that adaptive fragment vocabularies and VQ codes are
    plausible alternatives to BRICS, but without a verified artifact or
    direct PCQM result it remains a paper-level inspiration.
31. PG-MLD makes the privileged-information boundary explicit: dynamic 3D and
    local electronic signals can be distilled into a SMILES student, but the
    trajectory, charge, external-data, and no-PCQM-Gap contracts make it a
    low-priority post-selection teacher question rather than an architecture
    result.
32. ConforFormer supplies the cleanest new geometry-pretraining objective in
    this continuation: molecular identity can define positive pairs across
    conformers, and frozen-probe versus fine-tune evaluation can be separated.
    Its external RDKit/MMFF/OMol geometry must be replaced by ETKDG before a
    same-database adaptation is even considered.
33. CardinalGraphFormer isolates a different local mechanism: a query-gated
    unnormalized sum over the same sparse support preserves dynamic support
    cardinality, and explicit-size controls show why a raw graph-size shortcut
    is not enough. The result is ADME/2D evidence, not PCQM Gap evidence, and
    the reported 28M-corpus cost is far outside the bounded screen.
34. Contrastive KERMT shows that a probabilistic graph-to-SMILES objective can
    complement, but not replace, local atom/bond/functional-group supervision;
    the paper's cMIM-only ablation is weak. Its “unit weighting” is a modeling
    convention rather than equal gradient scaling, and its transductive corpus
    variants must not be copied into a sealed PCQM role.
