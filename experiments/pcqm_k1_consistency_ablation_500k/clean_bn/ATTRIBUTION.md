## Supported discriminator

An identical BN-buffer-only intervention improves both selected predictors
by approximately1.07/1.25meV with positive paired-row intervals, without changing
learned parameters. Therefore inference running statistics are one demonstrated
source of recoverable error on this consumed cohort. This is not evidence that
the consistency penalty uniquely supplies that gain. Its incremental benefit
remains0.119meV raw and0.293meV after both-arm calibration, neither material.

Native60-epoch exposure and selected-state restoration pass, so a truncated
training attempt or accidental weight update does not explain this endpoint.
Both arms use two training forwards: this comparison cannot measure the cost
or accuracy equivalence of removing the second forward. Removing the penalty
alone is not a measured twofold speedup.

## Insufficient evidence

This experiment does not identify the complete cause of100K-to500K shrinkage or
full-data ordering. Training seed variation, independent-role transfer,
capacity/readout, pretraining exposure and training-time normalization effects
remain insufficient_evidence. Train trace fields include the scaled optimization
objective, not matched clean inference train MAE; do not infer over/underfitting
from its difference to development MAE. Development was repeatedly selection-used.
No protected evaluation was performed to fill these gaps.

Normalization merits distinct future examination only under a new authorized
question. Native T4 phase timings remain the missing cost discriminator;
local CPU diagnostic timing is not a T4 training-speed estimate. No automatic
new screen or full-run release follows this attribution.

Reference identity is a separate prospective infrastructure defect. Canonical
trace recovery preserves observed history but cannot reconstruct an absent
prelaunch reference declaration. Fix storage honestly, not scientific history.
