# PairToken Kunshan batch-profile decision

Decision date: 2026-09-17

## Outcome

Kunshan job `122388380` completed the six-batch, two-cohort FP32 sweep in
`0.2003 DCU-hours`. All 12 outputs and their hashes passed no-inference
mechanical acceptance. No development, official validation, test-dev,
test-challenge, or shadow role was read, and no model/checkpoint was retained.

The BS128 representative result was `549.70 graphs/s`, within `0.4%` of the
completed 500K training mean (`551.74 graphs/s`), validating the profiler at the
authoritative contract batch. Loader/collate and H2D together contributed less
than 1% of measured step time. Forward and backward/optimizer dominated, so
more workers or graph-cache changes were not supported as the main remedy.

BS2048 was the fastest single-pass representative result at `643.20 graphs/s`,
only `1.17x` BS128, while reserving 54.2% of device memory. The sweep was
non-monotonic: BS256 and BS512 were substantially slower than BS128, and the
largest-graph cohort benefited from homogeneous shapes and very few measured
steps. Without order-randomized repeats, this did not establish a reliable
batch optimum.

## V5 decision

Execution and artifacts were accepted, but profiling coverage was `PARTIAL`:
the task did not time train-role evaluation as a validation surrogate, did not
serialize/hash a real model checkpoint, and did not repeat configurations in
randomized order. No scientific result or contract change was authorized.
Physical BS128 therefore remained authoritative.

Compact evidence is in `v5_acceptance_122388380.json`; raw profiles remain in
ignored platform record storage.
