# Bounded compute decision — 2026-09-28

One seed42 Kaggle2 T4x2 screen was selected to test chemistry-separated local
propagation against an equal-capacity bond-type control. The decision followed
the [GPS++ full-depth negative](../pcqm_k1_gpspp_local_100k/decision.md),
the [nonportable relation-resolution audit](../pcqm_k1_relation_resolution_100k/audit/decision.md),
and the [joint reconstruction nonpromotion](../pcqm_k1_joint_atom_reconstruction_100k/decision.md).
Those results rule out interpreting added local capacity or an original-role
score alone as a portable improvement; they do not test the one-layer delayed
mixing mechanism. The machine evidence `training_contract.json` and
[protocol](protocol.md) cap this release and keep all protected roles sealed.

No scientific endpoint, promotion, second seed, 500K training or full run was
established by this prospective decision. The selected hypothesis is an
MMGNN-inspired adaptation, not a faithful reproduction of that model.
